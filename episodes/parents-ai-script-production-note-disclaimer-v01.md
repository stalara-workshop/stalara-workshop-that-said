## Short spoken disclaimer

> This episode is not a substitute for your school’s policies, your child’s clinician, or emergency support. If something is urgent, unsafe, abusive, exploitative, self-harm related, or crisis-related, this podcast is not the place to stop. Get real-world help.

## Description-box disclaimer

This episode is for general education and conversation, not medical, legal, therapeutic, school-policy, or emergency advice. If a child is in immediate danger or dealing with self-harm, abuse, exploitation, threats, coercion, blackmail, sexual pressure, or crisis, contact real-world support right away: a trusted adult, clinician, school counselor, local emergency services, or a crisis line.

## Tone note

The disclaimer should not sound like scary legalese. The tone should be calm, direct, and protective: “This episode can help you start better conversations, but urgent safety situations need real-world support.”
